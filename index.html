FAQ

Questions people ask before hiring us

Is the price one-time or monthly?

It depends on the service, and every quote says which. Websites, online stores and apps have a one-time price. Ads management, SEO and care plans are monthly. Nothing renews or repeats unless your quote says "monthly".

How do payments work?

We agree the scope and price in writing first, and work starts once the booking payment is received. Jobs under ₹15,000 are paid upfront. Websites and stores are 50% to book and 50% before launch. Apps, custom builds and any other project above ₹1,00,000 are paid in four milestones (30%, 30%, 30%, 10%), each due after you approve the stage before it. Projects above ₹3,50,000 are split into six smaller stages about a month apart, so you never pay far ahead of the work. Monthly services are billed in advance.

Will my price go up later?

A one-time price never changes for the scope we agreed. A monthly price is locked for 12 months; after that we review it once a year and give you 30 days' written notice of any change. For ads management, the fee moves to the next tier only if your ad budget grows past your plan's limit for two months in a row.

What if I want changes after we've agreed the scope?

Each package includes two rounds of revisions. Anything new, such as extra pages, screens or features, is quoted in writing at ₹1,000 per hour, and we only start once you approve it. You will never find an extra on your invoice that you did not agree to.

What is not included in the price?

GST where applicable, your ad budget, and third-party costs: domain, hosting, paid themes or plugins, payment gateway charges, and app store accounts (Google Play is a $25 one-time fee and Apple is $99 a year, both set by them). We bill these at cost with no markup, or you can pay the provider directly.

Is ad spend included in your packages?

No. Your Google and Meta ad budget is paid by you directly to the platform, so you keep full control and see every rupee. Our fee covers setup, management and reporting. For a local business we suggest an ad budget of at least ₹15,000 a month.

What happens after my website or app goes live?

Bug fixes are free for 30 days after a website launches and 60 days after an app launches. After that you can manage it yourself or take an optional care plan, from ₹1,999 a month for websites and ₹4,999 a month for apps.

What does the care plan include? Does it cover new features?

The care plan keeps what we built running: software and security updates, backups, uptime monitoring, bug fixes, and up to 2 hours a month of small content edits such as text, images and prices. For apps it also covers updates needed for new Android and iOS versions and app store rules. It does not cover new pages, new features or redesigns; we quote those separately before starting. Hosting and domain charges are also separate.

Who owns the website, code and accounts?

You do. Once the project is fully paid, the custom code, designs, domain and admin access are transferred to you.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No honest agency can, because Google controls its rankings. What we do promise is proven SEO work and a clear monthly report showing rankings, traffic and leads.

Can I cancel a monthly service?

Yes. Monthly services have a short minimum term (2 months for ads, 3 months for SEO and care plans) because results take time to show. After that you can pause or cancel with 15 days' written notice before the next billing date.

Can we work together if we're in a different city?

Yes. Everything from the first call to launch and monthly reports happens over video calls, WhatsApp and email, so it doesn't matter where your business is based.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We build websites and apps for businesses and agencies abroad, quote in USD, GBP or AED, and accept international bank transfer or Wise. Ask us for the international price list.
